        04 — Our Story

        Kaafi means enough.
        Zyada means more.

        KAAFI ZYADA is a contemporary clothing brand that brings together bold self-expression and a distinct sense of Indian identity.

        We grew up on hand-painted cinema hoardings, cassette covers, Sunday-evening films and grandmothers’ trunks full of prints. KAAFIZYADA takes that colour and character and cuts it into silhouettes you actually want to wear today — confident, fresh and effortlessly stylish, with a playful edge and a refined finish.
